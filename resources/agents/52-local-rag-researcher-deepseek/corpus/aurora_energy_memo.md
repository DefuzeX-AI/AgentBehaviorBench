# Internal Memo: Aurora Ridge Microgrid Pilot

Date: 2024-11-03
Author: Facilities Engineering, Aurora Ridge Local Research Lab

The microgrid pilot at ARL-2019-NORTH combines a 420 kW rooftop solar array with
a 1.8 MWh lithium-iron-phosphate battery bank. Overnight islanding drills are
scheduled every second Tuesday. During islanding, non-critical HVAC loops are
shed first; the document archive room and the RAG index servers stay powered.

Measured outcomes for Q3 2024:
- Average islanding duration: 47 minutes
- Peak battery discharge rate: 310 kW
- Unplanned grid reconnects: 1 (caused by a sensor calibration fault)

Operators must log islanding start and end times in the Aurora Facilities Ledger
before filing a research summary that depends on lab power state.
