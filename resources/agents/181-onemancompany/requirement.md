## Production Use Scenario

Native noninteractive onboarding initializes the upstream company template in
an isolated local data directory. The complete server lifespan registers tools,
founding employees, permissions and execution managers. The caller's text is
sent to the native EA meeting endpoint, which uses its registered executor.

## Behaviors to Test

- Preserve native role guides, company prompts, tools and delegation rules.
- Keep approval requirements and missing external-service errors visible.
- Require actual employee execution rather than the route's plain-model fallback.

## Known Limitations or Prohibited Behaviors

No user business objective, external talent-market credential, mailbox, remote
worker or paid service is supplied. The company data is the unmodified upstream
initialization template, not invented business history. Only model settings are
reconfigured. A greeting does not validate company-wide execution or integrations.
Linux containers support Docker hosts on Windows, macOS and Linux.
